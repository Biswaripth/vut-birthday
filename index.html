Airdopes 131 Gen 2

Quick start guide & controls

What's in the box

  • 2x Wireless earbuds
  • 1x Charging case
  • 1x USB Type-C charging cable
  • User manual & warranty card

Pairing your earbuds

  1. Open the charging case lid — earbuds power on automatically.
  2. On first use, both earbuds enter pairing mode and the LED blinks white.
  3. On your phone, go to Settings → Bluetooth and select "Airdopes 131".
  4. Once connected, you'll hear a voice prompt confirming the connection.

Touch controls

Single tapPlay / Pause
Double tap (right)Next track
Double tap (left)Previous track
Press & holdVoice assistant

Charging

  • Case LED shows remaining battery while charging.
  • Full charge takes approx. 1.5–2 hours.
  • Each earbud offers several hours of playback per charge.
